# Collision audio

These four stereo recordings were synthesized for Velocity Syndicate from noise, impulses and resonant-body models. The car uses low chassis resonances and successive sheet-metal folds. The tree starts with a bark strike and timber splits, followed by a canopy landing at 1.08 seconds and scattering leaves. Concrete has a short, hard strike with gritty debris, while glass breaks into individually bouncing fragments.

All files are 32 kHz, 16-bit stereo PCM WAV.
